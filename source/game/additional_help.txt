SOMETHING ABOUT CLUTTER


Look at folder %APPDATA%/LOVE to save some space! 
This folder is for starting directly from code.
 

And look at folder %APPDATA%/gamename(eg. thousandoaks) or simply %APPDATA%/game. 
This folder is for starting from the compiled executable.


In Linux look for these
$XDG_DATA_HOME/love/ or ~/.local/share/love/
love may be replaced by gamename(eg. thousandoaks) or simply "game"


If you corrupt your settings and the game looks wonky, try deleting
the "options" file so it is regenerated to its default values.
