SOMETHING ABOUT CLUTTER


Look at folder %APPDATA%/LOVE to save some space! This folder is 
for starting directly from code.
 

And look at folder %APPDATA%/gamename or simply %APPDATA%/game. This folder is 
for starting from the compiled executable.


In Linux look for these
$XDG_DATA_HOME/love/ or ~/.local/share/love/
love may be replaced by game name or simply "game"
