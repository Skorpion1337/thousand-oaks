Use W, S, A, D to move around in the map.

Every turn the mines produce resources from the nodes in their area like volcanoes, mountains or forests. And from the monsters that live in them.

Every turn you can build roads to connect the cities for moving resources for example. The bigger the total population, the more roads you can build per turn. Every structure like volcanoes, mountains and trees counts as road so you can move through them.

Building a new mine costs 100 oak, it is built from one mine where it takes the oak from. So put your first mine next to some forests and trees. Trees give oak slower than forests.
